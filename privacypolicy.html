Privacy Policy

Contact Form Privacy Notice

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Who We Are

  • Data Controller: Áine Brady, Séimh Counselling and Psychotherapy, registered Psychotherapist/Counsellor in Ireland.
  • Professional Body: IACP Pre-accredited Member M15337
  • Contact Email: aine@seimhpsychotherapy.ie

2. What Personal Data We Collect

When you use our website contact form, we collect:

  • Identification Data: The name, email address, and phone number of the person completing the form.
  • Special Category Data: Any sensitive details regarding mental health, symptoms, or life circumstances that you choose to type into the message field.
  • Minor Data: If you are inquiring on behalf of a child or teenager under 18, we may collect their name, age, and brief details of the nature of the enquiry.

3. Purpose and Legal Basis for Processing

  • Purpose: We use this data strictly to evaluate your initial enquiry, determine suitability for our service, and contact you to arrange an initial consultation.
  • Legal Basis (Standard Data): Processing is based on your explicit consent when submitting the form and taking steps to enter a potential therapeutic contract.
  • Legal Basis (Special Category/Health Data): Under GDPR Article 9(2)(h), we process health data for the explicit purpose of providing healthcare or therapeutic treatment.

4. Special Rule for Children under 18 (U18s)

  • Parental/Guardian Consent: In Ireland, parental or legal guardian consent is required to process the data of a child under 18 for counseling services. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or legal guardian to complete this form on your behalf.
  • Information Minimisation: Please keep details in the text box to an absolute minimum. We only need basic contact info and a high-level summary of your request. Specific personal histories should be reserved for the safety of our first session.

5. How Your Data is Handled and Secured

  • Secure Email Routing: When you click submit, the form securely routes the contents directly to our password-protected, encrypted professional email inbox.
  • Access Control: Only the Data Controller has access to this email inbox. No data from this form is permanently stored on the website's public server database.

6. Confidentiality and Legal Limits

All form submissions are treated with strict professional confidentiality. However, in compliance with Irish law and the Children First Act 2015, confidentiality must be broken if:

  • There is a clear and immediate risk of serious harm to yourself or someone else.
  • A child protection or welfare issue (including historical abuse) is disclosed. In such cases, we are legally mandated to report these concerns to Tusla (Child and Family Agency).

7. Data Retention

  • If you proceed to therapy: The contact form data will be integrated into the client's clinical record and will be kept securely for 7 years after therapy ends. For minors, files are securely kept for 7 years after they turn 18 (until their 25th birthday), in accordance with professional insurance and guidelines.
  • If you do not proceed: All email submissions and correspondence will be permanently and securely deleted from our inbox within [e.g., 30 days].

8. Your Rights

Under the GDPR, you (and your child, depending on their age and capacity) have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of personal data. To exercise these rights or to raise a query, please email us directly. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Data Protection Commission.
